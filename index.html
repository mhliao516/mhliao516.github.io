Biography

I'm a postdoctoral researcher at Hong Kong Baptist University, advised by Prof. Xin Huang. I received my PhD in Computer Science from Beijing Institute of Technology in June 2026, where I was advised by Prof. Rong-Hua Li. Previously, I received my B.Eng. in Computer Science and Engineering from Beijing Institute of Technology in 2020.

Research Interests

My general research interest lies in spectral graph theory, numerical linear algebra, graph data management, and graph machine learning. I am interested in efficient implementations of theoretically fast algorithms, especially for large-scale and dynamic graphs.
